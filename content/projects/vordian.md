#### Vordian

Vordian helps coaches prioritise client reviews, inspect source evidence and ask questions about their client data. AI summarises and explains evidence from client check-ins, helping coaches assess suggested next steps and make informed coaching decisions. The preview shows the Today decision desk with fictional demo data.
